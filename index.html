Everything a small landlord actually needs

Not a spreadsheet. A real rent ledger.

Every part of running rental properties day to day — built specifically around how landlords and their staff actually work.

Buildings, flats & tenants

Track as many buildings and flats as you manage, each with its own tenants, rent amounts, and due dates — all in one clear view.

Automatic rent status

Every flat is automatically marked Paid, Overdue, Partial, or Advance each month — no manual tracking, no spreadsheets to update.

Bilingual receipts & contracts

Generate professional, bilingual PDF rent receipts, deposit receipts, and tenancy contracts — ready to share over WhatsApp in seconds.

Deposits tracked properly

Security deposits are tracked as a separate liability — never mixed into profit — with full collection and refund records, including partial deductions for damages.

Advance rent, handled

Collect 2, 3, 6, or 12 months of rent upfront at move-in, and future months are automatically marked covered — no manual adjustments needed.

Post-dated cheques

Track pending cheques separately from cash and bank payments, with simple one-tap marking for cleared or bounced cheques.

Staff accounts with real limits

Give trusted watchmen access to specific buildings only — they can collect rent and log expenses, without ever seeing your overall profit or salary data.

Full cash reconciliation

Know exactly how much cash each watchman should be holding at any moment — rent collected, deposits in and out, expenses paid, and cash already handed over, all netted automatically.

Works without signal

Record a payment with no internet connection — it's saved safely on the device and syncs automatically the moment connectivity returns.

Built around two very different jobs

Owners and staff see exactly what they need

Property Owner

The full financial picture

  • Profit, expenses, and salaries across every building
  • Deposits held as a liability, kept separate from profit
  • Cash reconciliation for every watchman, at a glance
  • Invite staff instantly by SMS, email, or in person with a one-time code
  • Generate bilingual contracts and receipts anytime, not just at move-in
Watchman / Staff

Focused on the job at hand

  • See only the buildings they're actually assigned to
  • Record rent payments and general expenses on the go
  • Check their own "cash on hand" figure before handing money over
  • Send WhatsApp payment reminders to tenants directly
  • No visibility into profit, salaries, or other buildings — by design
